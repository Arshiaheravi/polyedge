What is Polymarket?
Polymarket is a website where people bet real money on whether things will happen — sports results, election outcomes, whether Bitcoin hits a price target, and thousands of other events.

Every question has a YES and NO side. The price is between 0¢ and 100¢ — it represents the crowd's opinion of the probability. If you're right when it resolves, you get paid 100¢ per share.
💰
How do you make money?
Two ways:

1. Be right — bet YES or NO, wait for the event, collect 100¢ if correct.

2. Sell early — if you buy YES at 30¢ and the price moves to 70¢, you can sell now and pocket the difference. Like trading stocks, but for predictions.

Complete Beginner Guide

How to Copy a Winning Bettor

From zero knowledge to placing your first copied bet — step by step.

1
Create a Polymarket account
Go to polymarket.com and sign up. You can use your email (easiest) or a crypto wallet if you have one. It takes about 2 minutes.
💡 What is Polymarket? It's a website where you bet real money on real-world events — sports, politics, crypto prices, etc. Think of it like a stock market, but for predictions.
2
Deposit money (USDC)
Polymarket uses USDC — a digital dollar that is always worth exactly $1 USD. You deposit it like topping up a prepaid card.
Easiest way: On Polymarket, click Deposit → choose Credit/Debit Card → buy USDC directly with your card. Done.
💡 Start small — $20–$50 is enough to try it. You can always add more later.
3
Follow a winning bettor on PolyEdge
Go to the Leaderboard tab. You'll see the top 100 most profitable bettors on Polymarket ranked by real profit.

Pick someone with consistent profit — not just a big number today, check Month and All Time too. Click Follow next to their name.
💡 Tip: A high profit with moderate volume is better than huge volume with tiny profit. It means they're accurate, not just gambling a lot.
4
See their open bets in My Follows
Go to My Follows. You'll see cards showing every bet that bettor currently has open — markets still live, money still in play.

Each card shows: what they bet on, which side (Yes/No), current odds, their profit/loss, and when it closes.
5
Click the card → place the same bet
Click any position card. It opens that exact market on Polymarket. You'll see a page like this:
Example: "Will OKC Thunder win the NBA Finals? — YES at 45¢"
1. You land on the Polymarket page for that market
2. Click YES (or whatever side the bettor is on — shown on the card)
3. Type how much you want to bet (e.g. $20)
4. Click Buy — done. You're in the same bet.
💡 The price in ¢ is the probability. Betting YES at 45¢ means you think there's a 45%+ chance it happens. If it resolves YES, you get paid 100¢ per share — so $1 becomes $2.22. If it resolves NO, you lose what you put in.
6
Wait, sell early, or let it resolve
You have three options after placing a bet:
Hold until it resolves — wait for the event to finish. If you're right, Polymarket automatically pays you 100¢ per share. If wrong, you lose your stake.
Sell early to lock in profit — if the price moved in your favour (e.g. you bought YES at 45¢ and it's now 75¢), you can sell now and keep the difference. Go to Portfolio on Polymarket and click Sell.
Sell early to cut losses — if the price dropped (e.g. 45¢ → 20¢), you can sell now and recover some money instead of losing it all.
7
Withdraw your winnings
Once you have USDC in your Polymarket balance, go to Withdraw on Polymarket. You can convert back to regular money via a crypto exchange like Coinbase or Kraken — or just leave it in for the next bet.
⚠ Risk disclaimer: Prediction markets involve real money. Even the best bettors lose sometimes. Never bet more than you can afford to lose. Copy-trading does not guarantee profits — past performance of any bettor is not a guarantee of future results. Start small.
